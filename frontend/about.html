About

By The Way Cafe is a modern cafe located in Simpang Ampat, Perlis, offering a wide selection of delicious meals, homemade desserts, and refreshing beverages. Despite being a newly established café, it has quickly become a popular destination for both locals and visitors thanks to its cosy atmosphere, friendly service, and quality food.

Mission

To serve quality food and beverages with excellent customer service.

Vision

To become one of the most loved cafe by providing memorable dining experiences.
